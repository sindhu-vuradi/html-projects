Our Mission

The mission of 360digrii in SR Nagar is to bridge the gap between academic education and industry requirements by transforming graduates into "industry-ready" professionals. Established in 2011, the institute focuses on providing high-quality technical training in fields like Java and Python Full Stack development to combat unemployment and empower students with practical, need-based skills. Their goal is to foster long-term career growth through a holistic approach that combines technical expertise, real-time project experience, and dedicated placement support in top MNCs.
